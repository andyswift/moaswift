Smeeze gets the Cheese

It is 2am, there is a Comté on the floor and the mice know it.

  • Grab the cheese before the mice nick it and drag it down their holes.
  • Glowing things are yours to grab. Anything with a dark outline is furniture: walk around it. Mice can run under the sofa, bed and tables.
  • Set traps where they run. Early mice love the bait. Clever ones learn. Don't step on your own.
  • Guinness gives Smeeze Dutch courage: he boots any mouse he bumps into, but he is slow and he staggers.
