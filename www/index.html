A little studio in your browser.

A mixing console that listens to the other tabs. Open the synths and the drum machine on their own pages, press Start audio on each, and their sound arrives on the mixer's channel strips, ready for EQ, effects and a mix. It all runs live in your browser, with nothing to install.

Your tabs, wired up

Every instrument page sends its sound to the mixer over the browser itself. Close a tab and the strip waits for it to come back.

A proper mixer

Eight mono channels and four stereo inputs with EQ, six aux sends and three stereo buses, plus reverb, delay, granular, a bus compressor that ducks to the kick, and Ouroboros.

Plays with MIDI

Connect a MIDI keyboard or controller in Chrome or Edge. It follows MIDI clock too.

Open the mixer

MOA-1.

A mono synthesizer with a big keyboard, patch memories and a front panel full of things to turn. Play it on its own, or let it send its sound to the mixer in another tab.

Fat and simple

One voice, done properly. Bass, leads and squelch.

Remembers your sounds

Save your own patches and they are still there next time you visit.

Plays with MIDI

Play it from a keyboard, or let MOA-SEQ play it from another tab.

Play MOA-1

MOA-6.

A polyphonic synthesizer with six voices, chorus and echo, and patch banks to browse. Hold a chord and let it ring, or send it to the mixer and put it in a mix.

Six voices

Chords, pads and stabs, with the oldest note stepping aside when you run out of fingers.

Chorus and echo

Wide, warm and a little wobbly, the way a pad should be.

Patch banks

Browse the presets, tweak them and save your own.

Play MOA-6

MOA-DR.

A drum machine with six voices and a sixteen-step sequencer. Kick, snare, clap, two hats and a ride, each with its own tuning, decay and level. In the mixer every voice gets its own channel.

Six voices

Every drum has its own knobs, and the hats choke each other like they should.

Step sequencer

Sixteen steps, patterns A and B, swing and tempo, or just follow MIDI clock.

A channel each

Send the voices to the mixer in another tab and the kick can duck the bass.

Play MOA-DR

MOA-PIPE.

A synth with no oscillators. Noise is blown into a tuned pipe, and the pipe rings, sings or clangs depending on how you shape the feedback. Eight voices of Karplus-Strong physical modelling, plus a spring reverb to put it in a room.

Pluck, blow or strike

Sixteen patches from nylon strings and marimbas to pan flutes, reed organs and a steam drone.

Spring reverb

Length, tension, drip, tilt and dwell, with a dry/wet mix so it sits behind the pipe.

Plays with MIDI

Velocity, pitch bend, mod wheel and sustain pedal in Chrome or Edge, or just use the keyboard on screen.

Play MOA-PIPE

Lynch 106.

A six-voice polysynth in the mould of a classic analogue favourite: saw, pulse and sub oscillators, a resonant filter, one ADSR and a wide stereo chorus. A hundred and twenty-eight patch memories and a bender lever, drawn like the original panel.

Chorus I and II

Switch it off, pick either side or hold both for the fast, shallow shimmer.

128 memories

Two groups of eight banks of eight patches. Write your own into any slot.

The bender

Drag the lever to bend the pitch, push it forward for vibrato. It springs back like the real thing.

Play Lynch 106

MOA-DF2.

A dual dynamic filter box, inspired by a little hardware favourite and fed by the Lynch 106. Two filters with a gritty and a smooth circuit each, and both of them sing on their own when you turn the resonance up.

Eight filter types

Low-pass, band-pass, high-pass and notch, plus hybrids with two peaks. Pick one per filter.

Dynamic

The level of what you play can open the envelopes. Add a looping envelope, a sample-and-hold and a crunchy delay.

Play the Lynch

The polysynth sits on top: its left side feeds one filter and its right side the other.

Play MOA-DF2

MOA-DUO.

A paraphonic synth with two oscillators and a dual filter you can route a dozen ways, inspired by a compact analogue favourite. Play one note or two, sync the oscillators, loop the modulators and let the reverb ring forever.

Two voices in one

Hold two keys and each oscillator takes one. Sync them for the classic tearing lead.

Routable filters

Series, parallel, split or bypassed. Both filters sing on their own when you push the resonance.

Infinite reverb

Turn the decay all the way up and a chord hangs in the air until you change it.

Play MOA-DUO

MOA-SEQ.

A generative sequencer in the spirit of a favourite hardware one. Euclidean patterns, note repeats, probability, scales and chords, sixteen tracks at once, and it plays the other instruments on this site: open them in other tabs and they just listen.

Patterns that grow

Steps, pulses and rotation, on any division from whole notes to triplets. Different lengths per track make polyrhythms.

Alive

Repeats that speed up and ramp, chance, groove templates and micro-timing keep a loop from ever feeling pasted.

Plays your tabs

Lynch, Duo, DF2 and Pipe pick up the notes over a MIDI bus. Or send it out to real hardware.
